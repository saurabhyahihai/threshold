Privacy Policy

Threshold · Last updated: August 13, 2026
Threshold is an offline-first tarot application designed for card readings, tarot learning, and personal reflection. We value your privacy and have designed Threshold so that its core features can be used without creating an account or providing personal information to us.

1. Information Stored by Threshold

Threshold does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or other personal information.

Information created while using the app may be stored locally on your device so that Threshold can provide its features. This may include:

  • Tarot draws and reading history
  • Daily draw information
  • Cards explored in the guide
  • Statistics and progress
  • Gem balance and related app activity
  • App preferences and settings

This information is not transmitted to us by Threshold's core functionality.

If you uninstall the app or clear its application data, locally stored information may be deleted by your device's operating system.

2. Advertising

Threshold displays advertisements using Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

Although Threshold itself does not require personal information from you, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain information from your device and your interactions with advertisements and the app.

Depending on Google's current SDK configuration and applicable settings, this may include:

  • IP address
  • Advertising or other device identifiers
  • Information about interactions with the app and advertisements
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information used to provide, measure, maintain, and protect advertising services
  • General location information that may be inferred from an IP address

Google may use this information for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and improving its advertising services.

Threshold does not control information collected by Google through its advertising services. Google's processing of such information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

3. Rewarded Advertisements

Threshold may offer rewarded advertisements that allow you to receive in-app gems after attempting to view a rewarded advertisement.

Threshold does not require or store your personal information as a condition of receiving these rewards.

The advertising provider may process information associated with the advertisement request and viewing of the advertisement as described in this policy.

4. Third-Party Services

Threshold currently uses the following third-party service for advertising:

  • Google AdMob — advertising and related measurement, security, and fraud-prevention services.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing Google's privacy documentation to understand how Google handles data in connection with its advertising services.

Threshold does not sell your personal information.

5. Internet Connection

The core tarot functionality of Threshold is designed to work offline.

An internet connection may be required for certain third-party functionality, including loading advertisements.

If an advertisement cannot be loaded, the core tarot features remain available.

6. Data Security

Because Threshold's core user-created information is stored locally on your device, its security is also affected by the security of your device and operating system.

Information transmitted by third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads is handled according to their security practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Threshold is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through Threshold. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us using the address below.

8. Your Choices

You can choose whether to use rewarded advertisements when they are offered.

You can also manage certain advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google's available advertising controls. The availability of particular controls may vary by device, account, region, and applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Threshold's features, advertising services, or data practices change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy will be updated accordingly.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
